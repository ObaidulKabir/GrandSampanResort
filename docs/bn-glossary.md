## Digits and money

- Always Western digits: `ট12,34,567/-`
- Keep Bangladesh / Indian grouping (lakh-crore)

## Tone

- Short, concrete, action-focused Bangla
- Avoid stiff literal translations of marketing English
- Prefer verbs buyers understand over finance jargon
